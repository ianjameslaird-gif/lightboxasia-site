Lightbox FAQs

What is a fabric lightbox (SEG lightbox)?

A fabric lightbox is an aluminium frame lit from inside by LED modules, with a printed silicone edge graphic (SEG) pushed into a groove around the edge. The fabric pulls tight and glows evenly with no visible seams. They are used for trade show booths, brand backdrops, counters, towers and retail displays in Singapore.

Can I rent a lightbox in Singapore for one event?

Yes. Lightbox Singapore rents LED backlit fabric displays for single events, exhibitions, launches and activations across Singapore. Send us your event dates, venue and the space you have, and we will recommend frame sizes and a layout. Rental includes the frames with LED lighting, and we can produce the printed fabric graphics for you.

What lightbox frame sizes are available?

We stock 17 standard frame sizes, from a 1 × 1 m counter up to a 9 × 3 m wall, including 2.24 m-high walls, 2 × 2 m, 3 × 3 m and 6 × 3 m. Frames link together to build larger booths and backdrops. If a standard size does not fit your space, we can make custom-sized lightbox frames.

Can I buy lightbox frames instead of renting?

Yes. If you exhibit regularly, buying your own SEG lightbox frames is often better value. You keep the frames and swap only the printed fabric for each campaign. We supply frames, LED lighting and graphics, and can reprint new fabric for your existing frames through our sister service SEG Printing.
